Record Historical Payment

Enter the total amount received during a chosen date range before or while using Ezelex. The system splits the total across daily/weekly periods between the start and end dates, then preserves any amount above the scheduled target capacity as a clearly labelled excess credit on the end date. The total is never hard-coded. Review the preview before saving.

Choose a contract, enter the total amount received and the last date covered, then preview the weekly split.
