On-Credit Egg Sales

Record crates given to a customer who has not paid yet. Credit crates remain outstanding until the customer pays. Workers can edit credit details and record part-payments by crate quantity. Each paid crate is then counted as sold, its original cost is recovered into Reinvestment Cash, and its profit is recognised in the week the payment is received. Unpaid crates and their projected profit remain outstanding.
