Before You Apply

The EZELEX contract states that an employment offer is subject to satisfactory pre-employment checks and proof that the applicant is eligible and qualified to work within the partnership. Applicants also confirm that information provided during recruitment is complete and accurate.

The contract includes specific tricycle partnership/payment arrangements, responsibilities, change-of-agreement provisions and guarantor terms. Please read the full document before accepting any agreement.

A plain-language overview of key terms from the supplied contract. The full PDF is the authoritative document.

Employment offerSubject to pre-employment checks and proof of eligibility/qualification.
AcceptanceThe document says acceptance should be confirmed in writing and signed within 48 hours.
Option OneGH₵200 daily payment over 13 months, with the engine-oil arrangement stated in the contract.
Option TwoGH₵180 daily payment over 15 months, with the engine-oil arrangement stated in the contract.
Change / opt-outThe contract refers to 21 days' notice, an 8–12 month contact period, remaining balance provisions and a 15% penalty for certain early opt-outs.
OwnershipThe document states that the primary owner retains legal ownership of the tricycle until the final instalment is paid.
Loss / damageThe agreement places responsibility for specified losses and damages on the co-owner/rider and includes guarantor obligations.
Governing lawThe contract states that the agreement is governed by and consistent with the law of Ghana.
